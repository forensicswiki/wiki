**Intrusion detection** is the process of identifying attacks,
malware, and policy violations by matching network traffic and host
activity against signature rules or behavioral baselines. Intrusion
detection systems (IDS) may be network-based, inspecting traffic on
the wire, or host-based, monitoring activity on a single system.
