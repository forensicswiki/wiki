Academic and professional publications on the subject of digital forensics, both print and
electronic.

* A **journal** is a scholarly periodical that publishes peer-reviewed articles in a specific field
  of study, and is tracked by academic metrics such as the Impact Factor and H-Index.
* A **magazine** is a periodical that publishes articles for a broader audience, typically without
  a formal peer review process, and is not tracked by such metrics.

Part of the (original) information below originates from
[list of conferences and journals](https://digital-evidence.org/publish/index.html),
by [Brian Carrier](brian_carrier.md) and was copied with permission.
